Cookie-based affiliate tracking fails due to browser privacy protections and consent rejections. The article outlines three cookieless alternatives: server-to-server postbacks with click IDs, promo-code attribution, and first-party data subdomains, presenting Swetrix as a GDPR-compliant way to capture complete traffic data.
