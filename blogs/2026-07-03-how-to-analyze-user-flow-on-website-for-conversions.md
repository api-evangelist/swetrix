Traditional analytics platforms lose significant data due to cookie consent rejections and ad blockers, creating blind spots in conversion funnel analysis. The article advocates privacy-first, cookieless tools like Swetrix for capturing complete user journeys while staying GDPR-compliant, with frameworks for mapping flows and reducing friction using engagement rate and rage-click metrics.
